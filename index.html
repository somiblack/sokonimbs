What is Okoa Jahazi? Emergency credit service
Info ▼

Okoa Jahazi is Safaricom's emergency credit service that allows you to access airtime and data when you have insufficient balance.

  • 🔹 How it works: Dial *544# and select "Okoa Jahazi" to request credit
  • 🔹 Repayment: The borrowed amount is automatically deducted when you next top up
  • 🔹 Eligibility: Available to active Safaricom subscribers with good usage history

At SokoniMBS, we offer data bundles that you can purchase with or without Okoa Jahazi credit.
